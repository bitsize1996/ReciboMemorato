# Recibo Memorato one-page website

## Goal
Build a polished, visual-first one-page website for **Recibo Memorato by the bitsize sibs**, using the supplied copy and the line **“Because memories need proofs.”**

## Page structure
1. **Hero** — strong keepsake collage or receipt-style photo strip, concise opening copy, Messenger action, and the three-step supporting line.
2. **Problem** — spacious, emotional statement with subtle receipt-paper details.
3. **Solution** — introduce the “memory receipt” concept through perforated edges, date/time details, and restrained receipt typography.
4. **Services** — five photo-led product cards for Receipt Photobooth, Photobooth, High-Angle Photobooth, Photo Sintra Board, and Original Instax Printing.
5. **Why Recibo Memorato** — tactile uses for printed memories and a genuine founders/siblings photo.
6. **Social proof** — real customer/event photos and customer messages only; no fabricated testimonials. If none are supplied, use a clearly labeled gallery layout without invented quotes.
7. **Offer summary** — scannable list of available experiences and a Messenger action, without invented prices or packages.
8. **FAQ** — five supplied questions in accessible expandable rows.
9. **Final invitation** — emotionally focused closing statement and Messenger action.
10. **Footer** — brand lockup, service list, and working links to the relevant page sections.

## Visual direction
- Warm off-white canvas, clean black typography, and restrained red brand accents, adjusted to match the uploaded brand palette.
- Premium editorial composition with generous whitespace, crisp dividers, subtle paper texture, and occasional receipt-edge treatments.
- Real close-ups of physical keepsakes and candid event moments as the main visual language.
- Youthful details without making the page resemble an accounting receipt.
- Responsive layouts for phones and larger screens, with restrained motion and reduced-motion support.

## Assets and actions
- Use the uploaded logo, brand colors, product photos, customer/event photos, and founders photo.
- Optimize and place each supplied image according to its subject and crop.
- Point every “Send Us a Message” action to the supplied Facebook Messenger page URL.
- Add meaningful image descriptions and keyboard-friendly interactions.

## Content and discoverability
- Preserve the supplied English copy, tightening only line breaks and labels where necessary for readability.
- Add page-specific title, description, Open Graph metadata, and social sharing metadata for Recibo Memorato.

## Inputs needed during implementation
- Logo and brand files.
- Product, customer/event, and founders/siblings photos.
- Facebook page or Messenger URL.

If some images are not yet available, the corresponding areas will use tasteful neutral placeholders that are clearly ready for replacement, not invented customer proof.
